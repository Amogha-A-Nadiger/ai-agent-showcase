What AI can't do (yet)

Honest truth: AI agents are powerful, but they're not magic. Here's what they still need from humans.

  • ✕ They can't read your mind. You have to describe what you want. Vague instructions = vague results.
  • ✕ They make mistakes. Sometimes the code breaks. But they can also fix it — if you tell them what's wrong.
  • ✕ They don't have taste. They can build anything, but YOU decide if it looks good. Your judgment matters.
  • ✕ They can't replace you. They're a tool — like a calculator for code. The ideas still come from humans.
